Privacy Policy

Egelya Paylaşım · Last updated 20 September 2026

Egelya Paylaşım is a private content-publishing tool used by Egelya (egelya.com), a small olive oil and table olive producer in the Aegean region of Türkiye. It publishes Egelya's own marketing videos and images to Egelya's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What we access

When the account owner connects their TikTok account, we request only what is needed to publish:

  • Basic profile information — the account's display name, username and avatar, shown so the owner can confirm the correct account is connected.
  • Video publishing — permission to upload a video or photo to that account, together with its caption and privacy setting.

We do not read followers, messages, comments, or the content of other accounts.

What we store

Access and refresh tokens are stored in a local environment file on the owner's own computer. They are never transmitted to any third party and never leave that machine. Published videos and captions are Egelya's own marketing material, prepared before upload.

We keep no database, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, and no logs containing personal data. No data is sold, rented or shared.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the integration is in use and are deleted by removing the local environment file. The owner can revoke access at any time from TikTok's Settings → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions, which immediately invalidates the stored tokens.

Children

The tool is not directed at children and is used only by the account owner.
